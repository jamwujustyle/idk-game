# RONIN launcher artwork

`ronin.png` is the game's square launcher icon: an ivory katana over a vermilion
sun and blue-green water, on a charcoal tile with transparent outer corners.

The game entry created by `scripts/install-launcher.sh` references this image by
its absolute checkout path. Keep the asset in the checkout and rerun the installer
after moving the project. The separate Unreal Editor entry keeps its development
icon. Reopen applications search after reinstalling to refresh a visible result.

## Asset source

Original artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The PNG is the
unmodified generated output, including its alpha channel.

Generation prompt:

A premium, striking emblem with the clarity of a real game launcher icon, readable at 48 pixels. Composition: a large charcoal-black rounded-square tile occupying about 94% of the canvas, with genuinely transparent outer corners. In the tile, a single beautifully shaped ivory/silver Japanese katana rises diagonally from lower-left to upper-right, its long blade gently curved with a clearly defined small guard and short dark wrapped grip, in front of a large deep vermilion red sun. At the bottom, two broad simple muted blue-green water arcs cross the lower blade/grip area, suggesting a drowned landscape. Bold silhouette and sparse shapes; quiet Japanese woodblock influence, only very subtle brushed pigment texture; strong contrast, refined and dramatic. The sword is the unmistakable main subject. Flat straight-on emblem, no perspective, no mockup, no external scenery, no character, no gamepad, no additional swords, no busy ornament, no letters, no Japanese characters, no words, no watermark, no glossy mobile-game bevels. Crisp complete composition, generous internal breathing room but no large blank outer margin. Transparent background outside the charcoal rounded-square tile.
